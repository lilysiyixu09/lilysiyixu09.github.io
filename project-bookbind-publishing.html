Typography | Production

Bookbind Publishing

Maintaining brand consistency across global marketing materials meant closing a visual gap between our English and Chinese typography. The brand's headlines relied on a distinctive English typeface, but no Chinese typeface carried the same formal character — its proportions, weight, and personality simply had no equivalent in the Chinese type landscape.

To close this gap, I designed a custom Chinese headline typeface rooted in the structural and stylistic DNA of the original. This meant translating qualities like stroke modulation, counterforms, terminal treatment, and optical balance into a Chinese typographic structure — adapting Latin-script design logic to the fundamentally different visual grammar of Chinese characters, while preserving the brand's tone and legibility at headline scale.

The result: headlines that carry the same visual identity and brand recognition across English and Chinese markets, and a design language that stays cohesive across regions.
