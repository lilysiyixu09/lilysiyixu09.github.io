Global campaign localization

Back To School 2026

The global Back to School campaign was localized for the Greater China market and launched across Apple Store video. While preserving the original creative direction and Apple's global brand standards, the project required careful adaptation of typography, layout, and visual hierarchy to ensure the Chinese versions felt as natural and visually balanced as the original design.

Rather than simply replacing English copy with Chinese, this localization required meticulous typographic refinement to preserve the integrity of the original design. Through pixel-level adjustments to font size, weight, tracking, baseline alignment, and spacing, the Chinese layout was carefully adapted to maintain the original visual rhythm. The Traditional Chinese version was further refined with subtle optical adjustments, ensuring both language variants shared a consistent layout and delivered the same visual experience across the campaign.
