Campus Quarterly

This project focused on localizing a global content for the Apple, Greater China market, with a primary challenge in title redesign. As the original global design relied on a custom Latin type treatment, the localization required developing a Chinese title system that preserved the visual language and brand expression of the source typography.

To achieve consistency, I analyzed the original typeface’s formal characteristics, including stroke modulation, curvature rhythm, terminal treatment, and overall visual proportion, and translated these features into a Chinese typographic structure. Using geometric construction and circular guides, I established a consistent framework to refine stroke transitions, counterforms, and optical balance, ensuring both stylistic fidelity and legibility across characters.
